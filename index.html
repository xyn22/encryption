1 · Powers mod n and loops

RSA is one operation: me mod n. Everything else is about why doing it twice (once with e, once with d) brings m back.

  • You may reduce mod n after every multiplication. The answer does not change.
  • Each value is the previous one times a, mod n. Only n remainders exist, so the walk must repeat.
  • If the walk reaches 1, it restarts from the beginning. Exponents then only matter mod the loop length.
  • Exponents of the form (multiple of the loop) + 1 bring you back where you started.
